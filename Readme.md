## Superstore Sales Analytics

## Project Overview

Superstore Sales Analytics is an end-to-end Business Intelligence project developed using the Superstore dataset to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer segments, products, shipping modes, and regional performance.
The project uses Power BI to transform raw sales data into an interactive dashboard that helps identify sales trends, profitable products, high-performing states, customer segments, and shipping patterns.

## Business Objective
The main objective of this project is to answer key business questions such as:
    • How are sales and profit changing over time?
    • Which products generate the highest sales?
    • Which products generate the highest profit?
    • How does discount affect profitability?
    • Which sub-categories perform best in terms of sales and profit?
    • Which states generate the highest sales?
    • Which customer segments contribute the most sales?
    • Which shipping modes are used most frequently?

Business Insights
The dashboard can be used to identify:
    • Monthly sales and profit trends.
    • Products contributing significantly to overall sales.
    • Products generating higher or lower profitability.
    • Sub-categories with strong sales but comparatively lower profit.
    • States with high sales contribution.
    • Customer segments contributing to overall revenue.
    • The distribution of orders across shipping modes.
    • The relationship between discounts and profitability.
These insights can support decisions related to pricing, discount strategy, product management, customer segmentation, and regional sales planning.
